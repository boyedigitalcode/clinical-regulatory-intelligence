# Agent 03 — FDA Action Tracker 

## Purpose
This agent monitors FDA regulatory actions to track approvals, rejections, and advisory committee outcomes, providing insight into regulatory momentum and decision-making patterns.

It exists to answer:
**“What regulatory actions is the FDA taking, and what do these actions signal for development and approval strategy?”**

---

## Primary Data Sources
- FDA.gov Drug Approvals Database
- FDA Advisory Committee Meeting Documents
- FDA Press Releases related to regulatory decisions

---

## What “Change” Means
A change is triggered when:
- A drug or biologic is approved
- A Complete Response Letter (CRL) is issued (when publicly disclosed)
- An advisory committee meeting is scheduled or concluded
- Advisory committee briefing documents are released
- Advisory committee voting outcomes are published

---

## Key Analyses Performed
The agent performs regulatory outcome analysis by:
- Tracking approvals by:
  - Therapeutic area
  - Modality
  - Indication
- Analyzing advisory committee outcomes to identify:
  - Common regulatory concerns
  - Recurring objections or data gaps
- Identifying patterns in:
  - Approval timelines
  - Use of accelerated or expedited pathways
  - FDA risk tolerance across disease areas

---

## Outputs
- Real-time alerts for FDA approvals and major actions
- Summaries of advisory committee discussions and votes
- Identification of regulatory success and failure patterns
- Insights into shifting FDA expectations and risk thresholds
- Executive-level regulatory intelligence briefs

---

## Update Cadence
- Daily monitoring of FDA approval and action databases
- Event-driven updates aligned to advisory committee meetings
