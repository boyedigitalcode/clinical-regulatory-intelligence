# Agent 04 — Safety Signal Intelligence 

## Purpose
This agent monitors regulatory and scientific sources to identify emerging safety, compliance, and risk signals that could impact clinical development or regulatory outcomes.

It exists to answer:
**“Are there emerging safety or compliance risks that could affect clinical trials or regulatory approval?”**

---

## Primary Data Sources
- FDA Warning Letters
- FDA Safety Communications (when applicable)
- PubMed clinical and safety-related publications

---

## What “Change” Means
A change is triggered when:
- A new FDA warning letter is issued
- Warning letters reference:
  - Clinical trial conduct deficiencies
  - Data integrity issues
  - Safety reporting failures
- New scientific publications report:
  - Adverse events
  - Safety concerns
  - Mechanism-related risks
- Repeated safety themes emerge across multiple sources

---

## Key Analyses Performed
The agent performs safety intelligence by:
- Categorizing warning letters by:
  - Violation type
  - Trial phase
  - Sponsor characteristics
- Identifying recurring safety or compliance themes
- Mapping safety signals to:
  - Drug class
  - Mechanism of action
  - Patient population
- Assessing:
  - Severity of risk
  - Likelihood of regulatory escalation

---

## Outputs
- Early warning alerts for emerging safety concerns
- Summaries of relevant warning letters and publications
- Identification of high-risk trial designs or practices
- Risk-focused intelligence briefs for clinical and regulatory teams
- Executive summaries highlighting potential compliance exposure

---

## Update Cadence
- Weekly monitoring of FDA warning letters
- Continuous scanning of PubMed for relevant safety publications
