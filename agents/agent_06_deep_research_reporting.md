# Agent 06 — Deep Research & Executive Reporting 

## Purpose
This agent synthesizes insights from all intelligence agents to generate comprehensive research outputs, executive reports, and presentation-ready materials.

It exists to answer:
**“What do decision-makers need to know right now, and how should it be communicated?”**

---

## Primary Inputs
This agent consumes structured outputs from:
- Agent 01 — Guidance Intelligence Monitor
- Agent 02 — Competitive Trial Intelligence
- Agent 03 — FDA Action Tracker
- Agent 04 — Safety Signal Intelligence
- Agent 05 — Regulatory Pathway Optimizer

---

## What “Change” Means
A change is triggered when:
- A user requests a deep-dive analysis
- Significant new intelligence alters strategic conclusions
- Periodic reporting cycles are reached (e.g. monthly, quarterly)
- Leadership requires scenario-based analysis or briefing materials

---

## Key Analyses Performed
The agent performs research synthesis by:
- Integrating multi-source intelligence into coherent narratives
- Identifying:
  - Key trends
  - Strategic inflection points
  - Risks and opportunities
- Framing insights for different audiences:
  - Executive leadership
  - Board members
  - Investors
  - Regulatory and clinical teams

---

## Outputs
- Executive summary reports (PDF)
- Detailed research briefs
- Board- and investor-ready PowerPoint presentations
- Visual summaries of regulatory and competitive landscapes
- Decision-focused recommendations and scenarios

---

## Update Cadence
- On-demand report generation
- Scheduled executive reporting cycles
