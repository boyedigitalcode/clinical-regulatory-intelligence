# Agent 02 — Competitive Trial Intelligence 

## Purpose
This agent monitors global clinical trial activity to identify competitive dynamics, development trends, and strategic signals across therapeutic areas.

It exists to answer:
**“What are competitors doing in clinical development, and what does this indicate about the competitive landscape?”**

---

## Primary Data Sources
- ClinicalTrials.gov

---

## What “Change” Means
A change is triggered when:
- A new clinical trial is registered
- An existing trial changes status (e.g. recruiting, completed, terminated)
- Trial phase progression is observed
- Key protocol fields are modified, including:
  - Primary or secondary endpoints
  - Eligibility criteria
  - Enrollment size
  - Study locations
- Sponsor behavior changes (e.g. increased trial volume, geographic expansion)

---

## Key Analyses Performed
The agent performs competitive intelligence by:
- Tracking trial activity by:
  - Sponsor
  - Therapeutic area
  - Indication
  - Phase
- Identifying:
  - Acceleration or slowdown in development programs
  - Shifts in trial design strategies
  - Entry of new competitors or modalities
- Comparing sponsor behavior over time to identify momentum signals

---

## Outputs
- Competitive landscape summaries
- Identification of active and emerging competitors
- Detection of strategic shifts in trial design or execution
- Alerts for significant competitive movements
- Executive-ready insights highlighting risks and opportunities

---

## Update Cadence
- Daily monitoring of ClinicalTrials.gov updates
- Weekly synthesis of competitive trends
