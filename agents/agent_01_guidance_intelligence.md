# Agent 01 — Guidance Intelligence Monitor 

## Purpose
This agent monitors FDA guidance documents to identify regulatory changes and assess their impact on clinical trial design and regulatory strategy.

It exists to answer:
**“What changed in FDA guidance, and what does it mean for my clinical or regulatory strategy?”**

---

## Primary Data Sources
- FDA.gov Guidance Documents
- Federal Register (guidance announcements and notices)

---

## What “Change” Means
A change is triggered when:
- A new FDA guidance document is published
- An existing guidance document is revised
- Language changes affecting:
  - Clinical endpoints
  - Trial design
  - Patient populations
  - Statistical requirements
  - Safety expectations
- Draft guidance progresses toward finalization

---

## Key Analyses Performed
The agent performs analyst-level reasoning by:
- Comparing previous and current versions of guidance text
- Identifying:
  - Newly introduced requirements
  - Removed or reduced flexibility
  - Strengthened or softened regulatory language
- Mapping guidance updates to:
  - Therapeutic areas
  - Clinical trial phases
  - Drug and modality types
- Assessing:
  - Level of impact (low / medium / high)
  - Urgency of response

---

## Outputs
- Plain-language summary of guidance changes
- Impact assessment with rationale
- Identification of affected trial designs and programs
- Recommended next actions
- Executive-ready bullet points suitable for leadership briefings

---

## Update Cadence
- Weekly monitoring of FDA guidance publications
- Immediate alerting for:
  - Final guidance releases
  - High-impact revisions
