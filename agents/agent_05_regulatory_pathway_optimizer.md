# Agent 05 — Regulatory Pathway Optimizer 🛣️

## Purpose
This agent synthesizes outputs from all other intelligence agents to assess regulatory strategy options and recommend optimal development and approval pathways.

It exists to answer:
**“Given current signals, what regulatory pathway and development strategy maximize the likelihood of success?”**

---

## Primary Inputs
This agent consumes structured outputs from:
- Agent 01 — Guidance Intelligence Monitor
- Agent 02 — Competitive Trial Intelligence
- Agent 03 — FDA Action Tracker
- Agent 04 — Safety Signal Intelligence

---

## What “Change” Means
A change is triggered when:
- Significant new guidance affects development expectations
- Competitive dynamics materially shift
- FDA regulatory decisions indicate changing risk tolerance
- New safety or compliance risks are identified
- Multiple moderate signals converge into a high-risk or high-opportunity scenario

---

## Key Analyses Performed
The agent performs strategic optimization by:
- Evaluating potential regulatory pathways, including:
  - Standard approval
  - Accelerated approval
  - Breakthrough therapy designation
  - Orphan drug pathways
- Assessing trade-offs between:
  - Speed
  - Data requirements
  - Risk exposure
- Identifying common pitfalls observed in failed programs
- Recommending strategic adjustments to:
  - Trial design
  - Endpoint selection
  - Evidence generation plans

---

## Outputs
- Recommended regulatory pathway options with rationale
- Risk-benefit comparisons of alternative strategies
- Identification of potential regulatory obstacles
- Strategic recommendations tailored to program characteristics
- Executive-level decision support briefs

---

## Update Cadence
- Event-driven updates triggered by upstream agent changes
- Periodic strategic reassessment as new signals accumulate
